ABOUT
SheSafe is a smart women safety and emergency response platform designed to provide quick assistance during critical situations. The system connects an SOS smart bracelet, ESP32, GPS/location services, and a web-based emergency dashboard.When the user activates the SOS button, an emergency alert is sent to the authorized dashboard, helping responsible personnel or emergency contacts respond quickly.
Key Features
- One-Tap SOS Alert
- Live/Current Location Sharing
- Admin/Police Dashboard
- Secure User & Admin Access
